## 7. KEYWORD IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP

### Phase 1: On-Page SEO (Weeks 1-2)
**Files to Modify:**
1. **index.html** - Add comprehensive meta tags
2. **Home page** - Primary keywords (2-3 per section)
3. **Product pages** - Product-specific keywords
4. **New meta descriptions** - 155-160 characters each

### Phase 2: Content Creation (Weeks 3-8)
**Create Blog Posts for:**
- How water softeners work (informational)
- Water treatment solutions comparison
- Installation guides
- Maintenance tips
- Regional guides for each province

### Phase 3: Technical SEO (Weeks 2-4)
**Implement:**
- Structured data (JSON-LD)
- Schema.org markup (LocalBusiness, Product)
- Sitemaps (XML)
- Robots.txt optimization

### Phase 4: Local SEO (Weeks 4-6)
**Actions:**
- Google My Business setup/optimization
- Local citation building
- Region-specific landing pages
- NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone)

### Phase 5: Link Building (Ongoing)
- Guest posts on water industry blogs
- Local business directory submissions
- Partnerships with related services

## GLOSSARY

- **SERP** - Search Engine Results Page
- **CTR** - Click-Through Rate
- **NAP** - Name, Address, Phone
- **H1** - Heading 1 (most important heading)
- **Meta description** - Summary shown under title in search results
- **Long-tail keywords** - Longer, more specific keyword phrases
- **Schema** - Structured data markup for search engines
- **Backlink** - Link from another website to yours
